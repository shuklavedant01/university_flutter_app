## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intellectual gravity, institutional credibility, and forward-looking momentum of an elite global university. Designed primarily for high-utility, high-accountability mobile touchpoints—spanning admissions, academic transcripts, compliance, and grievance reporting—the aesthetic balances editorial rigor with contemporary digital precision.

The design movement combines **Corporate/Modern Institutionalism** with nuanced **Editorial Polish**:
- **Dignified Authority:** Grounded in collegiate tradition without feeling antiquated or bureaucratic.
- **Radical Accessibility:** Uncompromising legibility, large touch targets, explicit interactive states, and clear reading pathways compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA standards.
- **Calm Reassurance:** Complex administrative, financial, and confidential grievance workflows feel safe, orderly, structured, and deliberate.
- **Subtle Modernism:** Clean geometric layouts, micro-surfaces with razor-thin structural borders, and refined scholarly accents that evoke heritage without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative collegiate identity balanced by high-contrast functional utilities.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F2942`) — Deep Oxford Navy:** Anchors the hierarchy. Used for dominant brand surfaces, primary buttons, structural navigational chrome, high-contrast headers, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#8B1E3F`) — Collegiate Crimson:** Used selectively for priority academic notices, urgent compliance markers, key institutional actions, verification seals, and critical flags.
- **Tertiary (`#C5832B`) — Scholarly Amber:** Reserved for academic milestones, honor badges, attention-demanding notices, pending state indicators, and formal accent highlights.
- **Neutral (`#475569`) — Slate Neutral:** Dictates body typography, muted subheaders, and passive structural lines.

### Functional Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` provides a clinical yet warm paper-like foundation.
- **Card & Component Surface:** `#FFFFFF` pure white ensures optimal contrast and crisp content cards.
- **Muted Surface / Inset:** `#F1F5F9` delivers distinct separation for data groups, code blocks, and tertiary content blocks.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-clarity subtle boundary lines utilizing `#E2E8F0` for primary outlines and `#CBD5E1` for active or focused inputs.

### System & Status Accents
- **Success:** `#15803D` (Forest Green) for cleared compliance, GPA benchmarks, submitted forms.
- **Warning / Review:** `#B45309` (Burnished Ochre) for upcoming deadlines, academic advisories.
- **Destructive / Grievance Escalation:** `#991B1B` (Urgent Crimson) for grievance reporting alerts, errors, and disciplinary notices.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the literary prestige of **Merriweather** with the pragmatic precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings (Merriweather):** Reserved for academic titles, major section headers, formal credential badges, and institutional announcements. Merriweather confers historical permanence, high visual gravitas, and editorial sophistication.
- **Interface & Operational Text (Inter):** Leveraged for high-density forms, operational lists, status flags, compliance documentation, buttons, and navigation. Inter provides crystal-clear readability at compact sizes, even under varying ambient lighting conditions.
- **Numbers and Tabular Data:** Academic codes, GPAs, application IDs, and grievance case numbers must utilize `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` within Inter to maintain strict structural alignment across lists and tables.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system implements an exact 4px/8px modular base rhythm optimized for mobile devices, scaling gracefully to tablet displays.

### Form Factors & Grids
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile` (20px) screen boundaries and `gutter-mobile` (16px) separation between columns. Vertical stacking dominates to maintain uninterrupted cognitive focus during compliance and grievance workflows.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and 20px gutters. Allows structured split-views (e.g., grievance navigation tree on the left, submission details on the right).
- **Touch Targets:** All actionable controls (inputs, buttons, pill selectors, list items) strictly observe a minimum touch dimension of 48px by 48px, preventing misclicks in high-stress reporting scenarios.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Related micro-elements group within 4px to 8px. Form controls pair with labels using 6px. Sibling cards separate by 12px or 16px. Distinct thematic sections partition with 32px or 40px spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through a combination of **low-contrast borders** and **ambient low-opacity shadows**, avoiding heavy skeuomorphism in favor of tailored physical boundaries.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** `#F8FAFC` background. Completely flat; holds zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` coupled with a diffuse shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Elements & Dropdowns):** Subtle float for open menus, pickers, and active selection filters: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Sheets):** Critical compliance alerts, multi-step grievance sheets, and bottom confirmations: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`. Combined with an overlay scrim of `#0F2942` at 45% opacity with a 3px background blur.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** geometry that telegraphs institutional reliability, precision, and structural restraint. It consciously rejects exaggerated hyper-rounded styling to preserve academic seriousness.

- **Base Radius (4px / 0.25rem):** Applied to nested elements, internal indicators, tabular badges, and minor UI markers.
- **Component Radius (`rounded-lg`, 8px / 0.5rem):** Standard radius for interactive buttons, text inputs, selection pills, and dropdown trigger targets.
- **Card & Modal Radius (`rounded-xl`, 12px / 0.75rem):** Applied to standalone cards, alert banners, expandable accordions, and dialog surfaces.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, compliance state badges (e.g., "Verified", "Under Review"), and numerical avatar counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F2942`, typography `title-md` in `#FFFFFF`. Height: 48px. Border radius: 8px. Hover/Active: `#163A5C`.
- **Secondary (Accent):** Background `#8B1E3F`, typography `#FFFFFF`. Used for key submission checkpoints and grievance confirmations.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, 1.5px solid border in `#CBD5E1`, typography `#0F2942`. Active background tint: `#F1F5F9`.

### Form Inputs & Accessible Fields
- Built for strict clarity in sensitive workflows (grievance forms, financial filings).
- **Height & Padding:** 48px height, 12px horizontal padding. Font: `body-md`.
- **States:** Default border `#CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF`. Focus creates an immediate high-visibility double boundary: 1.5px `#0F2942` border with a 3px outer ring in `rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12)`.
- **Labels & Support:** Labels use `label-md` in `#0F2942` positioned above the input with a 6px gap. Mandatory fields feature a red asterisk (`#991B1B`). Assistive/Error messages utilize `body-sm` with at least 12px spacing from neighboring fields.

### Status Pills & Institutional Badges
- Compact indicators showing admissions standing, GPA tiers, and report resolutions.
- **Structure:** 6px vertical, 10px horizontal padding, `rounded-full`, text set in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Admitted / Cleared:** Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`, border 1px solid `#BBF7D0`.
- **Pending / Action Required:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
- **Urgent / Disciplinary:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.
- **Formal Institutional:** Background `#0F2942` with `#C5832B` text and hairline border.

### Structured Segmented Tabs
- Engineered for switching between dense sections like "Overview", "Requirements", and "Case History".
- Hosted in a `#F1F5F9` container with a 4px internal padding and 8px outer radius.
- Inactive tabs display `#475569` with `label-md` styling. Active tab smoothly transitions to an elevated `#FFFFFF` surface with crisp `#0F2942` text, accompanied by an ultra-subtle Level 1 ambient shadow.

### Academic & Compliance Cards
- Base container: `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 12px corner radius, Level 1 shadow.
- Header bands can optionally carry a 4px accent line on the left or top border (`#0F2942` for standard units, `#8B1E3F` for urgent compliance actions, `#C5832B` for honor rolls).
- Internal content separated by 1px dividers `#F1F5F9` to neatly divide transcripts, deadline timelines, and case updates.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px by 20px hit-surface within an enclosed 48px tap area.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`, `#FFFFFF` interior.
- Checked: `#0F2942` fill with crisp white checkmark or center dot. Ensures immediate binary certainty for legal compliance consent.
